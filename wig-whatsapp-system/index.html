WhatsApp Sales System

Sell wigs through WhatsApp with a smart assistant, while keeping full control of your business

What the system does

This system helps you sell women's wigs through WhatsApp in a simple, organized way. It has an online product catalog, a WhatsApp sales assistant that answers customer questions, and a dashboard where you can manage everything yourself.

The goal is to help you sell more wigs while still talking to your customers personally when it matters.

How customers buy wigs

Customers can reach you through the WhatsApp sales assistant in a few simple steps:

  • Browse or ask questions - They can look through your wig catalog or ask about specific styles
  • Get answers - The assistant answers using your actual product information
  • Choose and order - They select their preferred wig, length, color, and place their order
  • Get personal help - If needed, you can step in and talk to them directly

How the WhatsApp assistant helps

  • Answers common questions - Price, availability, styles, lengths, and more
  • Shows product photos - Displays the right wig with pictures and details
  • Helps compare wigs - Explains differences between different styles
  • Builds orders - Guides customers through selecting options and placing orders
  • Handles preorders - Records requests for wigs that are not currently in stock

How the owner stays in control

You don't have to let the assistant handle everything. You have full control of your business:

  • Take over chats anytime - Step in personally when a customer needs special attention
  • Manage all products - Add, edit, change prices, update stock, and manage photos
  • View all orders - See every order and update its status
  • Handle preorders - Review requests and decide which ones to accept
  • Talk to customers directly - When you take over, you chat with them yourself

How preorders work

If a customer wants a wig that you don't have in stock, you don't have to lose that sale:

  • Customer requests it - The assistant offers to record their request
  • You see it in your dashboard - All preorder requests come to you with customer details
  • You decide - Accept, decline, or keep it pending
  • Guide your stock - See what styles customers want most before buying new stock

Note: Preorders are just requests until you accept them. Payment is only discussed after you confirm and agree on next steps.

Why this can help the business

  • Save time - The assistant handles routine questions 24/7
  • Look professional - Customers see photos, prices, and details organized nicely
  • Never miss a sale - Capture requests for wigs you don't currently have
  • Stay personal - Step in anytime to build trust with customers
  • Make smarter decisions - Know which styles people want before restocking
  • Everything in one place - Products, orders, and conversations all managed together
